The paperwork

Your certificate 🎓

Finish every lesson in a school and it prints your name on something. It is drawn right here in your browser from the progress already saved on this device: nothing is uploaded, no account is needed, and the only person who decides what name goes on it is you.

Checking your progress…

🖨️ Printing it "Print" opens your browser's normal print dialogue with just the certificate on the page, so "Save as PDF" there gives you a clean file. Landscape looks best. "Download" saves it as a PNG image instead.
🤨 Is this worth anything? Not to an employer, and anybody telling you otherwise is selling something. It is worth exactly what the work behind it was worth, which is quite a lot: you read every lesson, ran every example and finished the projects. Put it on a wall, not on a CV. The thing that goes on the CV is the code you wrote in the workshop.
