The Insult Compiler ⚔️

Swordfighting, the Monkey Island way: you win not by swinging harder but by knowing the right comeback. Here every insult is a real Python error message, and every riposte is the fix. Land the right one and you strike; flail and you take a hit.

🗡️ Why a duel? Reading an error and knowing instantly what it means is the single most useful skill a beginner can have, and the thing that most separates a four-second fix from a twenty-minute panic. So we turned it into a fight. Win a bout to earn the Insult Duelist achievement; win without a single miss for Sword Master.
📖 Want the reference? Every error type here is explained properly in Lesson 10: Reading Errors Without Fear. The duel is the reflex training; the lesson is the understanding underneath it.
